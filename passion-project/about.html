Hatsune Miku is a Japanese virtual singer. Essentially, she is a voice bank software developed by Crypton in 2007 using vocal samples from a Japanese voice actress. The digital vocal software was then designed into a character, the iconic blue-pigtailed girl. Anyone can purchase a copy of her voicebank and freely create music using her voice. Some of my favorite Miku producers include: hachi, powapowa_p, wowaka, kikuo, and takeponG! All of Miku's music (and most of her costume designs) are created by her fanbase. As is the case with most of the winter designs featured in the homepage, fans also have the opportunity to enter contests to get their costume designs made into officially licensed figures. Miku has transcended far beyond her original medium. She performs worldwide in hologram form, accompanied by a live band, at an event called Miku Expo. (I have had the opportunity to attend a few of these concerts!) As you can see from this website, she is also a beloved collectible character and has quite a devoted fanbase of merchandise collectors, such as myself!
